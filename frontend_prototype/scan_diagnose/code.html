info Pathology Overview

Caused by the fungus Cercospora arachidicola, early leaf spot typically manifests 30-40 days after crop emergence. Favored by high humidity and warm temperatures, it leads to premature defoliation and significant yield loss if left untreated.

symptoms Identified Symptoms
  • Dark brown lesions with yellowish halos on upper leaf surface
  • Elongated necrotic spots on leaf petioles and stems
  • Lower leaf drop starting 2 weeks post-infection
